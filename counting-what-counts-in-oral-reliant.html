One million was not a lie. It was a translation.

Counting in missions is not a Western imposition on oral peoples. It is a native oral practice, and Scripture models, formats, and regulates it. What breaks down is not the impulse to count. It is the instrument we use and the paradigm we carry.

Phases of Engagement

Walk the questions in order

The scoring rule is simple enough to hold in your head: walk the questions in order, and the first time the answer is no, the group is in the phase before that. Pick a group you know and answer honestly. Your answers assemble into a spoken report.

The right metric for the scale

Precise counts belong in small rooms

A precise count of believers is the right metric when there are 120 in one room. It is the wrong metric when the question is whether a province has heard. Drag the scale and watch the right question change.
