Theory of constraints

One worker, three times the output

Eliyahu Goldratt made his name on a factory floor by moving a single worker from a fast station to the slowest one. No new people, no new machines, no new hours, and the whole line tripled. You have one spare worker. Place them.

Faithfulness is not the question.

The question is whether you have found the one place the system is blocked, and whether you have the discipline to aim there and nowhere else until it gives. Naming a bottleneck is a diagnostic act, not a claim of control. Paul planted, Apollos watered, and God gave the growth.
