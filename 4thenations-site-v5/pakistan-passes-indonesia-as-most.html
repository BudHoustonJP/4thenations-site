Pakistan now has more people in primarily Muslim people groups than Indonesia. Joshua Project’s current data puts Pakistan at 251,369,200 and Indonesia at 251,021,080. That is close enough to flip with one revised estimate.

But a group label is not a headcount. Count the Muslims inside each group, and the near tie becomes a gap of about 15 million, and 13.5 million Christian adherents come into view. Scroll through, and work the controls.

Why it matters

The count shapes where prayer, money, and workers go

If the count rests on group labels alone, it can blur where Muslims actually live and where the church is already present inside Muslim communities. Open each action. The bar fills as you go.

Raw people group totals treat everyone in a primarily Muslim group as Muslim. Weighted counts multiply each group’s population by its recorded share of Islam. That turns a near tie into a lead of about 15 million.

Primary religion means the faith with the most adherents in the group. In Indonesia, 18.5 million people inside primarily Muslim groups are not Muslim, and about 13.5 million of them are Christian adherents.
