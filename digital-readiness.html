How to read it

What it shows

Four World Bank indicators for each country, with the year of each figure. Not every country reports every year, so years differ.

The strategy tag is a starting read, not a verdict. Move the thresholds to match your own field experience.

Joshua Project figures: Unreached is 2% or fewer Evangelical and 5% or fewer professing Christian. Frontier is 0.1% or fewer professing Christian.

Read it carefully

  • Mobile subscriptions count SIM cards, not people. Many people carry two, so the figure can pass 100.
  • Many wealthy countries no longer report adult literacy. When it is missing, the tag uses internet use alone and says so.
  • Being online is not the same as finding the gospel online in your heart language.

Sources

World Bank, World Development Indicators: Individuals using the Internet (IT.NET.USER.ZS), Mobile cellular subscriptions (IT.CEL.SETS.P2), Adult literacy rate (SE.ADT.LITR.ZS), Urban population (SP.URB.TOTL.IN.ZS).
