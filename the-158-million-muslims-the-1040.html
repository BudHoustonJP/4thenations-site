The 10/40 Window misses 158 million Muslims.

If the Muslims outside the 10/40 Window formed one country, it would have the fourth-largest Muslim population on earth, just ahead of Bangladesh.

Joshua Project’s current people group data tests an assumption mission strategy has leaned on since 1990, and the assumption holds. Of roughly 2.09 billion Muslims in the world, more than 1.9 billion (92.5%) live inside the 10/40 Window.

The Window will need many more workers, sent from every continent, for a long time to come. This piece is about the 7.5% it leaves out.

    About the numbers. Joshua Project people group data as of 8 November 2026. For each of the 16,419 people group in country (PGIC) records, the population was multiplied by its recorded percentage Muslim, and the results were summed. The 10/40 Window here is Joshua Project’s revised list of 68 countries. Counting every member of a Muslim-majority people group as Muslim puts 94% inside the Window, because that method misses Christians inside Muslim groups and Muslims inside mixed groups such as the Yoruba of Nigeria. A rough cross-check against Pew Research Center’s 2020 regional estimates also lands near 93%.
