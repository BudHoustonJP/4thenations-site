How to read it

What it shows

For each destination, the easiest short-term entry any selected passport gets, from visa free down to no admission.

"No visa in advance" counts visa free entry, visa on arrival, and electronic travel authorizations such as the US ESTA.

People group counts use Joshua Project thresholds. Unreached: 2% or fewer Evangelical and 5% or fewer professing Christian. Frontier: 0.1% or fewer professing Christian.

Read it carefully

  • Entry is not access. A visa free week is not a residence permit.
  • Team mode shows the best passport for each country, so one teammate may be the only one who gets in easily.
  • Territories (Puerto Rico, Greenland, and others) follow their governing country and are not in the entry data.
  • Frontier is shown by the percentage test only. Joshua Project also checks for known movements, so its own counts can differ slightly.
