Two numbers

5,413 workers needed. 123 million believers next door.

Joshua Project estimates 5,413 cross-cultural workers are needed to reach every frontier people group on the continent. Now count the evangelical believers already living in just four countries.

Set the two numbers side by side and it comes to roughly one worker for every 23,000 African evangelicals. Africa does not lack people. It lacks awareness, mobilization, and equipping at scale.

The engagement gap

Two-thirds of the belt has no known worker

Counted as distinct peoples rather than country by country, the belt holds 300 frontier groups. Tap each segment of the bar.

194 of 300 peoples sit at Engagement Phase 0. For roughly two-thirds of these peoples, no cross-cultural worker is known to be focused on establishing self-sustaining churches.
143

For 143 of these people groups, the need is exactly one cross-cultural worker. A single family, prepared and sent, closes the engagement gap for an entire people.

Partly a gap in activity, partly a gap in our knowledge: engagement already championed by African believers has not all reached Joshua Project yet.
