We blamed the mountains. The data says the barrier was never the altitude.

Innsbruck waited nearly four centuries for its first Protestant church, and the Alps seemed like the obvious reason. They were not. A test across thousands of people groups shows what terrain actually does: it protects whatever people already hold.
